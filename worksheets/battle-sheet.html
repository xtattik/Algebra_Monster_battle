Work out every attack — five steps

  1. Choose your attack — magic, strike or agility — and write its equation, e.g. 3x + 1.
  2. Add your character bonus — Magic on a magic attack, Agility on an agility attack: High +x · Average 0 · Low −x. Strike: none.
  3. Add the environment — does it Boost +x or Hinder −x this attack's type? Strike: none — but a Tests: Strength card still applies (step 4).
  4. Add the enemy's defence — their Strength (High −x · Average 0 · Low +x), plus the environment's Tests: Strength term if it has one (check your own Strength the same way).
  5. Collect like terms into one nx + c. Roll the die, substitute. Base attack min 1; after bonuses min 0. Subtract from the enemy pet's HP →

Every bonus is an x term — +x, −x or 0, never a plain number like +2.
