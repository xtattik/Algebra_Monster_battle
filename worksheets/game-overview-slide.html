Algebra Monster Battle — Quick Reference

Keep this on screen during play

Before the Battle

  • Draw one environment card — read what it does to each attack type.
  • Both players choose a pet, then reveal together.

On Your Turn

  1. Choose your attack — magic, strength, or agility.
  2. Add your character bonus.
  3. Add the environment bonus.
  4. Simplify → one nx + c.
  5. Now roll the die.
  6. Substitute your roll.
  7. Calculate damage → subtract from the enemy pet's HP.

Remember

  • First turn: rock‑paper‑scissors, a coin flip, or a dice roll.
  • Choose and simplify before you roll — not after.
  • No attack type three turns running — mix it up (you don't have to use all three).
  • A terrain Cancel means that attack type can't be used at all this match.
  • Every bonus is an x term: +x, +2x, −x, −2x — never a plain number.
  • Damage floors at 0; a base attack never deals less than 1.
  • Win: roll a 6 to capture the beaten pet; your pet heals to full either way.
  • Lose: no elimination — head to the second-chance bracket and keep playing.
