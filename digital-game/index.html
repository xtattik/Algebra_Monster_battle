Algebra Monster Battle

Pick a character, roll two random pets, trade one, draw an arena — then fight it out by building a linear expression, collecting like terms, and rolling a die to substitute for x. No maths, no damage.

Pets

Pets attack with unexpanded expressions like 3(x − 2) or −4x + 9 — expand first, then collect terms as usual.

How a turn works
  1. Pick an attack type — magic, strength or agility — from your fielded pet.
  2. Add your stat term (+x High · 0 Average · −x Low) and the arena's term for that type.
  3. Collect like terms into a single nx + c — you have to get this right before you can roll.
  4. Roll the die, substitute it for x, and the total (floored at 0) comes off the enemy pet's HP.
